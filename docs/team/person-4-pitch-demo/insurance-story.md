# Insurance Buyer Story

Insurance companies care about the first days after discharge because that is when confusion can become avoidable utilization.

CAREFLOW creates value by making the patient recovery plan clearer:

- fewer avoidable readmissions from missed red flags or follow-ups
- better adherence to medication and recovery instructions
- fewer confusion-driven support calls
- clearer caregiver coordination
- future aggregate risk signals for care teams without changing the patient-first workflow

The buyer message is not that CAREFLOW replaces care teams. The message is that it makes discharge instructions easier to follow, which helps insurers support safer recovery and reduce preventable cost.
