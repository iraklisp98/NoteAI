# Future Android Plan

The Android version should reuse the same backend and recovery plan contract.

Future workflow:

1. Patient scans or imports discharge paperwork.
2. Backend extracts text and runs the same agent pipeline.
3. Android displays the recovery dashboard in patient-friendly sections.
4. Push notifications remind the patient about medications and follow-ups.
5. Chat remains grounded in the generated recovery plan.
6. Caregiver sharing lets a trusted family member follow the plan.
7. Insurance and care team dashboards can later track aggregate adherence and risk signals.

Do not build Android during the hackathon. Present it as the credible next phase after the localhost MVP proves the workflow.
